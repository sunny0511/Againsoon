# Privacy Policy

**Againsoon** (“we”, “the app”)
Last updated: 16 September 2026

Againsoon is a couples date-planning app. In this version, your account and couple data are stored **on this device**.

## What we collect
- **Account:** email, first name, and a hashed password stored in the device keychain / secure storage (or browser local storage on web).
- **Couple data:** meets, calendars (mocked), wishlists, lists, memories, location-sharing preference, and optional photos you attach to memories.
- **Location (optional):** if you turn on hour-before location, the app uses GPS on this device to estimate how far you are from a meet. Location is not uploaded to a server in this version.
- **Place search:** if you search for a venue, the query is sent to OpenStreetMap-related geocoding services (Photon / Open-Meteo / Nominatim) to show a map pin.
- **Assist:** if you ask for date ideas, the prompt may be sent to a cloud language-model API (Pollinations by default, or an endpoint you configure). If that call fails, ideas are scored on-device.

## What we don’t do (this version)
- We do not operate a cloud account server.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show ads or use tracking SDKs.
- We do not use the Google Advertising ID.
- We do not use your data for third-party advertising.
- On Android, Againsoon is excluded from device backups so couple data is not copied into Google backup.

## Sharing
Your invite code and couple data stay on this device unless you screenshot, export, or share them yourself. Joining with a code on this phone switches the local profile.

## Retention and deletion
Delete your account in **Us → Delete account**. That removes the account, session, and couple data stored by Againsoon on this device.

## Children’s privacy
Againsoon is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
